# Figure index

`run_figures.sh` is the executable panel list. Outputs are written under
`out/`; the table below records how they enter the paper.

Panels that require non-distributed keypoint-MoSeq results or cleaned
keypoints are reported as `SKIP` rather than failed. Their coverage is listed
in [`DATA_AVAILABILITY.md`](DATA_AVAILABILITY.md#artifact-coverage).

```bash
bash reproduce.sh analysis
bash reproduce.sh figures
```

| Paper figure | Command target | Generated material | Final layout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Figure 1 | `fig01` | Panels a and c–f | Manual; panel b is a schematic |
| Figure 2 | `fig02` | Panel a and the panel-b components | Manual |
| Figure 3 | `fig03 assemble` | Panels a–d | Scripted in `figures/fig03/compose.tex` |
| Figure 4 | `fig04` | Panels b and d | Manual; panel a is a representative source-frame montage and panel c is a schematic |
| Figure 5 | `fig05` | Eleven data subpanels | Manual |
| Figure 6 | `fig06` | Panels a–e | Manual |

Manual means that the scripts regenerate the data-derived source panels placed
in the published layout; they do not reproduce panel letters or
typography-only assembly fragments.
